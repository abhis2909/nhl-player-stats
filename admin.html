Projection Methodology

Controls how the Stats page's Next-Season Preview and Rest-of-Season projections are computed. Season weights are how much each of the 4 seasons before the projected one counts toward a player's weighted-history baseline (must sum to 1). The clip range bounds how far the age curve is allowed to move that baseline up or down, in case a thin sample at the young/old tails overreacts. Shrinkage controls the Rest-of-Season view specifically: how many real games it takes before this season's own actual rate starts outweighing the modeled one — lower trusts a hot/cold start sooner, higher waits longer for a bigger sample.

Rating Methodology

Controls the percentile-based "overall" rating engine behind Player Ratings, Range Ratings, Team of the Week, and the Stats page's Power Ranking column — all four read this same configuration. Position weights control how much each stat counts toward a skater's overall (goalies use one shared profile). Eligibility is the minimum fraction of the pool's max games played to be rated at all. Tier thresholds must stay strictly descending (Diamond > Amethyst > Ruby > Emerald > Gold).

Jersey Pack Art

Upload a jersey photo, pick its team, give it a name, and classify its rarity — the background is removed right here in your browser (nothing uploads until you click Publish). Rarity class is what drives pack odds: a pull picks a random player weighted by their class's odds (see the odds strip on packs.html), so a card's tier is fixed to that specific player rather than rolled independently — pick it deliberately, it's not cosmetic. Publish to GitHub commits the processed PNG straight to this repo's jerseys/ folder. That's the image only, though — it doesn't wire the player up to it: copy the snippet below into packs.js's JERSEY_ART yourself (or hand it to Claude) to actually put it in the game.
