Card Design Preview — One Player per Tier

Six tiers now, each with its own gem, threshold, and effect intensity — not just color. Diamond 92+ (border shimmers fastest/brightest, pulsing badge, sparkle glints, diamond-cut tiles), Amethyst 87–91 (same pulse + sparkles, one notch down in intensity, octagon tiles), Ruby 82–86 (heptagon tiles, strong glow, no sparkles/pulse), Emerald 77–81 (hexagon tiles, moderate glow/aura), Gold 70–76 (circular tiles, slow faint shimmer, small glow, no background aura), Silver <70 (rounded-square tiles, static flat border, no shimmer/sheen/glow/aura at all — deliberately plain). Real players, one picked from the middle of each tier band on the live site. Not deployed anywhere yet — just this preview page.
