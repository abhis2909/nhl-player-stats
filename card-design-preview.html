Card Design Preview — Top 3 by Overall

Tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond) is driven by the overall rating bucket — Diamond 90+, Gold 80–89, Silver 65–79, Bronze <65 — so it lines up with the normal-distribution curve: most players land Silver, Diamond is genuinely rare. Team stays as a small badge rather than the whole card's color, so tier communicates "how good" at a glance and team stays identifiable but secondary. Border shimmers subtly on all tiers, more noticeably on Diamond. Not deployed anywhere yet — just this preview page.
