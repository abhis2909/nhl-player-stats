Card Design Preview — Top 3 by Overall

Tier now calibrated against the real (reverted-to-weighted-average) rating distribution — measured on the live 693-skater pool: min 28, max 89, mean 60. So: Diamond 85+ (~top 3%), Gold 75–84 (~next 17%), Silver 50–74 (the big middle, ~55%), Bronze <50 (~25%). Stat tiles are now shaped by tier too — diamond, hexagon, circle, rounded square — with just the number inside and the label underneath, unclipped. Photo now starts at the very top of the card with badges floating over it, instead of pushing the photo down. Not deployed anywhere yet — just this preview page.
